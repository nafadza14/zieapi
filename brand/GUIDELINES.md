# Apizie logo guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea:** a URL path, `//`, that ends in a data point. Every request lands on clean data. The magenta dot returns as the dot on the final "i" of the wordmark.
- **Versions:** horizontal (primary), stacked, symbol only, wordmark only.
- **Files:** `logo/*.svg` are the masters. `png/` has 1200 px exports and `icons/` has the favicon and app icon set. Every wordmark is converted to outlines, so no font is needed.

## 2. Construction
- Two capsules 40 units wide with a 75° slant (exactly 15° from vertical), on a 256 unit canvas.
- A dot with a radius of 24 units sits on the baseline and dips 2 units below it as an optical overshoot.
- The small-size cut (`apizie-symbol-small.svg`, used for favicons) is heavier: 52 unit strokes and a 30 unit dot.

## 3. Clear space
Keep a clear zone equal to **the diameter of the dot** around the logo on every side. It scales with the logo.

## 4. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 96 px wide | 25 mm wide |
| Symbol | 16 px (use the `icons/favicon` files) | 6 mm |

## 5. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Night ink | #0B0B14 | 11, 11, 20 | Slashes and wordmark on light backgrounds |
| Night | #05050A | 5, 5, 10 | Website and app-icon background |
| Signal magenta | #C23BEA | 194, 59, 234 | The dot on light backgrounds |
| Signal pink | #FF9ECF | 255, 158, 207 | The dot on dark backgrounds |
| Lilac | #B7A4FF | 183, 164, 255 | Gradient partner to pink on the website |

**Approved pairs:** full colour on white, white with pink dot on Night, all black on white, all white on any dark photo area.

## 6. Typography
- Wordmark: Inter Display SemiBold, outlined, tracked tight, with the last "i" dot in the accent colour.
- Website: Inter Display Light for headlines, Poppins for body text. Both use the SIL Open Font License.

## 7. Don'ts
Don't stretch, rotate or change the slant. Don't recolour the slashes. Don't add shadows, outlines or glows. Don't move the dot or change the spacing inside a lockup. Don't retype the wordmark in a font.

## 8. Before launch
I can't confirm trademark clearance. Run a search on the DJKI (pdki-indonesia.dgip.go.id) and WIPO databases, plus a reverse image search, before you register or print.
